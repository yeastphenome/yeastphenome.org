Background

Yeast is a model organism because it can reproduce (a process called "budding" to produce an almost identical offspring) in about 90 minutes [1]. This means that if a typical yeast can go through 30 [2] of these events before death, we can study the entire life (and aging) cycle of an organism in 2700 minutes, or just under two days. Yeast also has ~6,275 genes, of which 23% are homologous to humans [3]. This makes the organism a good model for studying aging in human, because we can age and perturb the yeast, and then test if discoveries are relevant to humans.
