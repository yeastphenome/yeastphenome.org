Explore data

Datasets

Search across more than 16K datasets to find data based on paper, phenotype, medium, or condition metrics. You can add one or more datasets to your download cart to generate a custom download, or navigate to papers to download entire sets. For eat dataset, you can then view dataset metadata, similar datasets, along with phenotypic scores.

Genes

Search across over 6K yeast genes, either by a name, alias, or other identifier. For each gene, you can then view metadata, most and least similar genes based on other datasets, and phenotypic scores.

Phenotypes

Search almost 7K phenotypes described by over 500 observable features. Query based on conditions, reporters used, and papers that published them.

Conditions

A condition can encompass everything from temperature to growth phase, and we encourage you to use the conditions browser to interactively explore condition counts across papers or datasets. For a search query, you can again search across phenotypes, tags, doses, and names.

Papers

Finally, you might just be interested in browsing literature first. The paper search queries across author names, phenotypes, and conditions to give you a list of matching papers to explore. From the detail view of a paper you can find the abstract, other paper metadata, and associated datasets.

Statistics

Curious about the overall contents of the database? You might be interested to browse the statistics page, which covers everything from counts for different entities to graphs for papers, phenotypes, environments, and datasets.
