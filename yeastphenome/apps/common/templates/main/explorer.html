Papers

The complete list of publications that report at least one genome-scale screen of the yeast knock-out collection. Criteria for inclusion are explained at Project.

Phenotypes

Quantitative (e.g., growth) or discrete (e.g., shape) traits that have been measured or observed across all knock-out mutants in any given screen.

Datasets

Each screen of the collection is a dataset that includes a set of tested genes, an experimental condition and an array of quantitative or discrete values describing the phenotype of each gene.

Conditions

Natural environments (e.g., nutrient availability, temperature shifts) and artificial perturbations (e.g., chemical compounds, expression of exogenous proteins) that act as stressors and reveal phenotypes.

Genes

All yeast ORFs (verified, uncharacterized and dubious) that have been associated with phenotypes across all datasets.
